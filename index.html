Description

14 Morton Street is an attractive mid-terraced property ideally positioned in the heart of Clonmel town centre. Having been recently renovated internally, the property offers well-proportioned accommodation arranged over three floors, together with a small yard to the rear.

The accommodation is particularly versatile, comprising a kitchen/living room and hallway at ground floor level, two bedrooms and a bathroom on the first floor, and a further two bedrooms and bathroom on the second floor.

The property benefits from mains services and enjoys a highly convenient town centre location, with an extensive range of shops, cafés, restaurants, schools, public transport links and local amenities all within easy walking distance.

The combination of its central location, four-bedroom accommodation and recent internal renovation makes 14 Morton Street an appealing opportunity for those seeking a town residence or an investment property.
